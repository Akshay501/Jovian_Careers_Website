About Jovian

At Jovian, we’re pioneering the future of interstellar data solutions—turning cosmic insights into actionable intelligence for a connected universe. Founded in 2022 by a team of astrophysicists, AI engineers, and visionary entrepreneurs, Jovian bridges the gap between cutting-edge space technology and everyday innovation.

Our mission is simple: Democratize access to the stars. We develop intuitive platforms that harness satellite data, machine learning, and quantum-inspired algorithms to solve real-world challenges—from climate prediction and urban planning to personalized education and beyond.

With headquarters in the heart of Silicon Valley and remote teams spanning four continents, Jovian fosters a culture of curiosity, collaboration, and bold thinking. We believe the next breakthrough doesn’t come from working harder—it comes from exploring further.

Join us. Let’s build the tools that will define tomorrow, today.
